Hello, I'm Joshua.

I'm an IT professional currently working as an engineer at Think Unified, a Managed Service Provider. In this role I manage complex, multi-client environments spanning Active Directory, Microsoft 365 security, firewall and VPN configurations, and endpoint management across 40+ organizations using NinjaRMM.

I've responded to live cybersecurity incidents involving the Termite and Akira ransomware groups, handling containment, investigation, and recovery. That real-world exposure to active threats is what solidified my drive to move deeper into cybersecurity.

Before IT, I spent years in management, turning around underperforming teams and hitting results under pressure. That background gave me communication, leadership, and problem-solving instincts that I carry into everything I do in tech.
